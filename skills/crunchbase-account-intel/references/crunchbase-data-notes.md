# Evidence notes

Use returned canonical identity, explicit dates, source URLs and observed coverage. Schema absence is not proof of a plan restriction. Authentication/permission failures are not empty datasets. Products and descriptions may disagree; prefer dated corroboration and disclose uncertainty instead of asserting all products are newer.

A similarity score is a candidate-finding aid, not proof of competitive substitution. Predictions are provider estimates, not independent facts or purchase intent. Growth score is about growth; heat is media/market attention and does not establish hiring, health or decline. AI-generated summaries are leads; ground factual claims in structured records or cited evidence and preserve source dates. Never upgrade record creation time to an occurred-event date.

Respect returned restriction tags without repeating restricted content. Disclose the material effect on coverage without claiming why the source omitted a value. Unknown amounts, dates and absent histories stay unknown. Keep CRM/user records separate from Crunchbase evidence. External research is used only when requested and attributed, never merged into deterministic Crunchbase scores.
